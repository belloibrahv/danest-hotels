# Staff guide

How to do the everyday jobs in the hotel system, by role. Sign in at **/staff** with the username and password the manager gave you. Everything you do is recorded with your name.

On a phone or tablet, open the account menu (your name, bottom left) and choose **Install app** to put it on your home screen.

---

## Everyone

- **Change your password:** your name → **Password & security**.
- **Two-step sign-in** (required for anyone who sees money): same page → **Turn on**, scan the code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator), and keep the recovery codes somewhere safe.
- **Your shifts:** **Staff & rota** shows this week; your own week is at the top.
- **Search anything:** press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) and type a guest's name, a room or a page.
- **"Live"** in the top bar means screens update by themselves. If it says *Reconnecting*, numbers refresh when the connection is back.

---

## Front desk

**A guest arrives with a booking**
1. **Overview → Arriving today**, or **Reservations**. Open the booking.
2. Check their photo ID and record its type and number.
3. Choose a room if none is assigned, then **Check in**.

**A walk-in guest**
1. **Room board** → tap a free room → **Walk-in guest**, or press **N** anywhere for a new booking.
2. Fill in the guest, nights and payment, then **Check in now**.

**Taking payment**
- Open the booking → **Record payment**: amount and method (cash, transfer, POS).
- A company guest whose bill goes to their company: in the booking open **Company billing**, choose the company and switch billing to the company on; at check-out the balance moves to the company's account.

**Charges to a room**
- Bar and food: the bar charges them at the till with **Room**.
- Gym, pool, lounge, laundry, room service, transport: **Facilities & services** → choose what, the amount, **Charge to room**, and the guest.

**Check-out**
1. Open the booking → check the bill (**Invoice** prints it or saves a PDF).
2. Take any balance owed. The system won't check out a guest who still owes money; only a manager can override that.
3. **Check out**. The room turns to *To clean* for housekeeping.

**Website requests**
- They appear on the **Overview** and with a number on **Reservations**. Call the guest, then **Confirm** (or cancel).

**Messages to guests**
- In the booking, **Messages**: choose the message (confirmation, reminder, balance, thank-you) and send by WhatsApp, SMS or email.

## Bar & shop

**Ringing up a sale**
1. **Bar & shop till**: tap items (or scan a barcode into the search box), choose how they paid, **Charge**.
2. **Room**: choose the in-house guest instead; it goes on their bill.
3. **Food** orders go to the kitchen screen by themselves. Add a table or name under **For the kitchen**.
4. **Print receipt** from the message that appears, or from today's list below the till.

**A mistake on the till**
- Tell the manager: only a manager can void a receipt, with a reason, on an open day.

**Stock**
- **Stock** shows what is left. *Reorder* and *Out* mean it is time to tell the manager.

---

## Kitchen

- **Kitchen** shows every food order from the till, oldest first, in three columns: **New**, **Cooking**, **Ready**.
- Tap **Start cooking** when you begin, **Ready to serve** when it is plated, and **Served** when it has gone out.
- A ticket turns red after 10 minutes waiting. New orders make a short sound once someone has tapped the screen.

---

## Housekeeping

- **Housekeeping** lists the rooms to clean, yours first.
- Tap **Cleaned** when a room is ready for the next guest.
- Something broken? **Report a problem**: what and where, and whether the room can't be sold until it is fixed.

---

## Manager

**Every day: close the day** (after the last sale)
1. **Close the day**. Sort out anything listed: guests who didn't arrive (no-show), guests past their check-out.
2. Count the cash drawer and enter the amount. The system shows what should be there: cash taken in, less cash paid out.
3. If the count differs, write why. Then **Close**. The day is locked against changes, and the owner gets the day's summary.

**Stock and buying**
- **Purchases → New purchase**: *Arrived now* when goods are in front of you, or *Place an order* to receive later.
- When an order arrives: open it → **Receive delivery**; change any line that differs from the order; choose how it was paid (now, or on credit).
- **Stock → Upload file** adds many items and their opening stock from a spreadsheet; **Export** gives the same layout back.
- **Stock → Insights** shows best sellers, items not selling, and what to reorder (with **Place orders**).
- **Count** an item to record breakages or shortfalls. Every write-off is shown to the auditor.

**Prices and rooms**: **Rates & seasons** for weekend and seasonal prices; **Settings** for rooms, the price list for facilities and services, taxes, and the website's wording and photos.

**Staff**: **Team** for sign-in accounts and roles; **Staff & rota** for everyone on the payroll and the weekly rota (tap a box to change a shift, then **Save**).

**Payroll** (each month): **Staff & rota → Payroll** → the accountant starts the month's pay → you **Approve** → the accountant marks it **Paid** once the transfers have gone. **Payslips** prints them all.

**Audit questions**: **Audit & controls → Queries** lists questions from the auditor waiting for an answer.

## Accountant

- **Accounts**: **Overview** (money in and out, cash and bank, who owes us, what we owe, the cash counts), **Cash book** (every naira, downloadable), **Owed to us**, **We owe** (pay supplier bills here), **Budget**, **Taxes** (for filing returns) and **Ledger export** (for QuickBooks, Sage or Xero).
- Record running costs under **Expenses**.
- Payroll: start the month's pay, adjust each person's allowances, overtime and deductions (PAYE is entered per person), and mark it paid after the manager approves.
- Answer the auditor's questions under **Audit & controls → Queries**.

---

## Auditor

- You can see everything financial and every action, and change nothing.
- **Audit & controls → Exceptions** collects what deserves a second look: voids, refunds, guests let out owing, cash differences, reopened days, stock written off, entries made days later, deliveries that differed from orders, access changes and failed sign-ins.
- **Ask** on any exception sends your question to management. Their answers come back under **Queries**; **Follow up** or **Close** when you are satisfied.
- **Full trail** is every recorded action, filterable and exportable. Your exports are recorded too.

---

## Owner

- **Executive** shows the month against last month and last year (same number of days), your targets and where the month is heading, the trend over a year, where bookings come from, the team's performance and what needs your attention.
- **Targets** sets the month's revenue, profit, occupancy and room-rate goals.
- **Settings → Backups**: backups happen every night; you can download one (your password is asked).
- At each close of day, the day's summary comes to the phone or email set under **Settings → Daily summary for the owner**.
